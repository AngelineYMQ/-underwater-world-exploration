Pilot Series

Four Saturday mornings, four different ways to explore.

The first series is designed for a small group so that everyone can ask questions, take part in activities and share what they notice.

Session 01

Marine Life Around Singapore

Meet local animals and habitats, and learn what makes Singapore's shores more alive than they first appear.

Saturday morning · 60–75 min
Session 02

What Not to Touch

Learn why some animals sting, bite or pinch, and why even harmless-looking wildlife should be observed carefully.

Saturday morning · 60–75 min
Session 03

Plastic, Microplastics & Food Chains

Use simple activities and examples to understand how plastic can move from our daily lives into the ocean.

Saturday morning · 60–75 min
Session 04

Become a Young Shore Explorer

Make a mini field guide and learn how to observe, record and ask questions without disturbing wildlife.

Saturday morning · 60–75 min
Who it is for

Small, curious groups.

Designed for children aged about 7–11 who enjoy animals, science, nature or simply asking lots of questions.

Session format

Group size: about 6–10 children
Length: 60–75 minutes
Fee: Free during the pilot series
Venue: Private residential function room in Singapore
Exact location: Shared privately after registration

What happens in a session

Not a lecture. A guided discovery session.

Look

Use photographs, species cards and real examples to notice details that are easy to miss.

Ask

Children are encouraged to ask questions and say what they think before hearing an explanation.

Make

Each session includes a simple hands-on activity, field card, mini challenge or take-home prompt.

Why I am hosting this

I learned more when I had to explain what I cared about.

I first shared about ocean animals and ocean pollution with younger students. Later, my PYP Exhibition helped me research microplastics and marine food chains more deeply. Saturday Ocean Club continues that journey by helping younger children discover the same questions in a more interactive way.
