Saturday Ocean Club · Ages 7–11

Come explore Singapore’s sea!

Do you like animals, science, beaches or discovering weird things that live in the ocean? Join a small Saturday morning club where we learn, play, make things and ask lots of questions.

I want to join! What will we do?
What happens on Saturday?

No long lecture. We learn by doing.

⭐

Meet sea animals

Learn about sea stars, crabs, corals, octopuses and other animals found around Singapore.

🔎

Guess & discover

Look at clues, pictures and mini challenges. Can you work out what the animal is?

🧪

Try simple activities

See how plastic can move through the ocean and why tiny microplastics are hard to spot.

✏️

Make something

Create a mini field card, puzzle, drawing or explorer checklist to bring home.

4 Saturday themes

Pick the one you like — or join all four.

Saturday 1

Who Lives Around Singapore?

Meet some cool local marine animals and learn where they live.

Saturday 2

Touch or Don’t Touch?

Which animals can sting, bite or pinch? Learn how to explore without hurting yourself or the animals.

Saturday 3

Where Does Plastic Go?

Follow the journey of plastic from land to sea and find out what microplastics are.

Saturday 4

Be a Young Shore Explorer

Learn how to look, record, ask questions and explore like a little marine scientist.

Who will be sharing?

A student explorer who loves animals, science and the sea.

I started by picking up rubbish on beaches, then learned scuba diving and saw plastic underwater. Later I shared about ocean animals and pollution with other students and explored microplastics for my PYP Exhibition. Saturday Ocean Club is my way of sharing what I have learned with younger children.
