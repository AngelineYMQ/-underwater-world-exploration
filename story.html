My Story

A love of science grew into a reason to look after the natural world.

My interest did not begin with one school assignment. It grew through reading, science, animals, travel, environmental habits and eventually the ocean.

Age 5 onward

Curious about nature

I was fascinated by the natural world and by how animals and ecosystems work. Wildlife documentaries and books kept giving me new questions.

Primary years

Small environmental habits

I began recycling bottles, saving paper and joining community activities. I learned that helping the environment could be part of everyday life.

Singapore

Living close to the coast

Moving to Singapore made the sea feel much closer. I started picking up rubbish on beaches and became more interested in what happens beyond the shoreline.

2024

Learning to dive

Scuba diving connected theory with practice: safety procedures, equipment checks, underwater hand signals and finally the real ocean.

Then

From observation to project

Seeing rubbish underwater led to more questions, a school sharing session, my PYP Exhibition on microplastics and eventually this website.

Future direction

Animals remain at the centre

My interest in biology, environmental science and animal welfare also connects with my long-term hope of becoming a veterinarian.

“I learned that curiosity is not only about knowing more. Sometimes, what I learn changes what I notice — and what I feel responsible for.”
